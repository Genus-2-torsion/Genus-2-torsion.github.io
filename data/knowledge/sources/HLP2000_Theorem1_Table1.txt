In this paper we present many explicit families of curves of g enus 2 and 3 whose Jacobians possess
large rational torsion subgroups. The strategy behind our c onstructions is to take a product of
elliptic curves, each with large rational torsion, and to ﬁn d a curve whose Jacobian is isogenous to
the given product. Thus it is no surprise that the groups we li st occur as torsion groups of abelian
varieties; rather, the point of interest is that they occur a s torsion groups of Jacobian varieties.
