Exclusions beyond the census

11 November 2026

The census lists groups with recorded curves. This page records proved exclusions for groups that have no census row. Each statement concerns the exact rational torsion group.

Six groups excluded for Jacobians split over ℚ

If a principally polarized abelian surface over ℚ is isogenous over ℚ to a product of elliptic curves and its rational torsion has order 256, then all of its 2-torsion is rational. Its torsion therefore has four generators. The following six groups are impossible for a genus-2 Jacobian split over ℚ, at every splitting degree.

Read the complete proof, or download the PDF and LaTeX source. The proof uses Mazur's elliptic torsion theorem, the Rouse–Zureick-Brown bound for 2-adic images, and a matrix argument uniform in the gluing degree. Supplementary exact matrix checks and their log are included.

This result makes no exclusion claim for geometrically simple Jacobians or for Jacobians that are simple over ℚ but split over ℚ̄.

What the order-256 theorem leaves open

The three groups of order 256 left by the theorem and the Weil pairing are [2,2,2,32], [2,2,4,16], and [2,2,8,8]. No existence assertion is made for these groups.

7-torsion and the 2-primary part for Jacobians simple over ℚ

A principally polarized abelian surface over ℚ that is simple over ℚ but geometrically split has rational 2-primary torsion of order at most four if it has a rational point of order 7. This theorem of 11 November 2026 applies at every geometric splitting degree.

It excludes every exact group with 7-torsion and 2-primary order at least eight in that class. For example, [56], which has no census row, is impossible there. The census group [2,28] is likewise impossible in the “geometrically split, simple over ℚ” column. Its split-over-ℚ case remains open. The theorem leaves [28] and [2,14] open in the ℚ-simple geometrically split class, and makes no exclusion claim for geometrically simple Jacobians.

Read the complete proof, or download its PDF and LaTeX source. The exact Magma checks and completed log are included.

5-torsion and the 2-rank for Jacobians simple over ℚ

A principally polarized abelian surface over ℚ that is simple over ℚ but geometrically split has rational 2-torsion of rank at most 2 if it has a rational point of order 5. This theorem of 11 November 2026 applies at every geometric splitting degree.

It excludes every exact group with 5-torsion and 2-rank at least three in that class. Besides the census groups [2,2,10], [2,2,20] and [2,2,2,10], this includes for example [2,2,40] and [2,10,10], which have no census rows. The theorem makes no exclusion claim for geometrically simple Jacobians or for Jacobians split over ℚ.

Its proof also shows, with no point of order 5 and no polarization hypothesis, that an abelian surface over ℚ that is geometrically the square of an elliptic curve without CM and whose endomorphism field is cyclic of degree 4 (Sato–Tate group E₄) has at most four rational 2-primary torsion points.

Read the complete proof, or download its PDF and LaTeX source. The Magma certificate and its log, and a supplementary check on known examples with its log, are included.

5-torsion and the 2-primary part for surfaces simple over ℚ

An abelian surface over ℚ that is simple over ℚ but geometrically split has at most four rational points of 2-power order if it has a rational point of order 5. This theorem of 14 November 2026 applies at every geometric splitting degree and assumes no polarization; it strengthens the 2-rank bound above.

It excludes every exact group with 5-torsion and a 2-primary part of order at least eight in that class. Besides the census groups [40] and [2,20], this includes for example [80], [2,40] and [4,20], which have no census rows. The bound is sharp: [2,10] occurs in this class. The theorem leaves [20] open in the ℚ-simple geometrically split class, and makes no exclusion claim for geometrically simple Jacobians or for Jacobians split over ℚ.

Read the complete proof, or download its PDF and LaTeX source. The Magma certificate and its log are included.

No point of order 45 for Jacobians simple over ℚ

A principally polarized abelian surface over ℚ that is simple over ℚ but geometrically split has no rational point of order 45. This theorem of 14 November 2026 applies at every geometric splitting degree; for surfaces that are geometrically the square of an elliptic curve it needs neither simplicity over ℚ nor a polarization.

It excludes every exact group containing an element of order 45 in that class: the census group [45], and for example [3,45], which has no census row. The theorem makes no exclusion claim for geometrically simple Jacobians or for Jacobians split over ℚ; the census lists split-over-ℚ examples of [45] and a certified infinite family.

Read the complete proof, or download its PDF and LaTeX source. The Magma certificate and its log are included.
